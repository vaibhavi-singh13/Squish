# SQUISH! - Bounce Back Adventure

A 2D platformer where you play Pip, a small blue slime. Five worlds, a boss at the end,
and a sky that goes from day to evening to night while you play.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser. No install, no server.

## Controls
- A / D or Left / Right: move
- Space / W / Up: jump (tap for a hop, hold for a full jump)
- S / Down: squish to charge a bigger bounce, then jump
- P or Esc: pause
- R: restart the level
- Enter: continue on the menu and story screens
- Esc on any other screen: go back

On phones and tablets you get on-screen buttons. You can hold several at once and slide
your thumb from one button to another.

## Files
- `index.html` - menus and HUD
- `style.css` - the look of everything outside the canvas
- `game2d.js` - the game itself
